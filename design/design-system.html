03 · Principles
Design Principles
Witness, don't prescribe
The app records what actually happened — not what should happen. No guilt, no gamification pressure. Data without judgment.
Data at a glance
Numbers are large and chromatic. Calories, protein, weight, and volume are instantly scannable without reading labels.
Friction-free logging
Every log action should take under 15 seconds. Defaults, search-to-fill, and optional fields keep the path clear.
Dark-first, focus-first
Dark surfaces reduce eye strain during early morning or late night logging. Accent colors carry all meaning.
Optional is truly optional
Photos, weight entries, and extra fields never block the core flow. Optional means hidden by default, revealed by intent.
Progress is earned, not inflated
Charts and streaks reflect honest data. A gap in the streak is fine — the app doesn't fill it in or apologise for it.
